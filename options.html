IRCTC passenger autofill

Saved only in this browser. On IRCTC, turn on the switch in the IRCTC Autofill panel on the right of the page. When you then click Book Now, this selects the passengers below from your master list, presses Continue, and stops on the payment page. You type any captcha and you pay, unless you turn on the panel's Complete payment switch: then it also pays from your IRCTC eWallet.
